# Limitations

- **Analytical target.** Financial pressure is a two-of-three accounting rule, not default, bankruptcy or an official rating. Scores support screening and ranking; they cannot justify lending, investment or supplier decisions alone.
- **Source verification.** Raw files are not included. Notebook 01–02 were statically checked, while supplied-panel derived fields were checked and 03–05 rerun. Strict duration and conflict-handling corrections in the extraction code have not been validated against the omitted raw files and could change a rebuild.
- **XBRL comparability.** Standard tags and USD reduce some inconsistencies but do not make concepts identical. Revenue component fallbacks, restricted cash, continuing-operations cash flow and alternative capex tags can have different scopes. Missing liabilities affect 6,320 observations. No values were invented to fill the published panel.
- **Selection and incomplete years.** SIC exclusions, minimum revenue/assets and at least three retained observations restrict applicability and may introduce selection or survivorship effects. Keeping all eligible observations reduces arbitrary subsampling, not selection bias. The 280 observations in fiscal 2024 are incomplete coverage.
- **Retrospective filing selection.** Latest annual 10-Ks are retained for company-years, amendments excluded. Filing dates and accession lineage are absent from the supplied panel. Financial statements were not necessarily available at the feature-year end, and temporal splits are not exact filing-date backtests.
- **Macro availability.** Annual FRED values are contemporaneous calendar-year averages joined by fiscal-year number, not lagged values or original release vintages. Non-calendar fiscal years are imperfectly aligned. Revisions and publication delays are not reconstructed.
- **Dependent observations.** The same companies can occur in training and test years. Repeated firms and shared economic shocks invalidate strict independence assumptions behind ordinary row-level confidence intervals, Welch tests and chi-square tests. Those outputs are descriptive. No claim of unseen-company generalization or causal effects is made.
- **Model scope.** Only numeric public financials and broad sectors are used. Correlated predictors complicate individual coefficient interpretation. A single temporal holdout and modest differences from persistence do not establish robust business value or production readiness.
- **Tied scores and capacity.** Tree leaves and persistence produce many tied scores. Stable score-descending, CIK-ascending ranking is reproducible but arbitrary within ties. Annual ceiling rules slightly exceed nominal review shares. Counts refer to company-years, not unique firms across the whole holdout.
- **Sensitivity only.** Scenarios shift selected ratios before applying unchanged preprocessing; other features stay fixed, so the result is not an accounting-consistent stress test. Fixed baseline score/CIK boundaries permit workload expansion. Scenario movement is not a forecast or causal conclusion.

Useful next checks would include a later holdout period, a company-grouped sensitivity check, filing-date and macro-vintage alignment, and review of the source tags behind heavily missing ratios.
